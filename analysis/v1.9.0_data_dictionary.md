# RDK Task - Data Dictionary

> Version 1.9.0

Release generated February, 2026.

Browse files: [https://github.com/Brain-Development-and-Disorders-Lab/task_rdk/tree/v1.9.0](https://github.com/Brain-Development-and-Disorders-Lab/task_rdk/tree/v1.9.0)

## Data

### General

- `trialNumber`: Number of elapsed trials, indexed from `0`.
- `score`: Total number of correct responses, counting `rdk-task`-type `main` trials only (not calibration or practice trials).
- `dotDirection`: The plaintext direction of the coherent dots, either `left` or `right`.
- `correct`: Whether the participant selected the correct direction of the coherent dots, either `1` or `0`.
- `activeCoherence`: The current proportion of coherent dots being presented during the moving dots stimulus. Example: A value of `0.13` indicates 13% of dots on-screen will be coherent, the remaining 87% will be random distractor dots.
- `coherences`: Generated low and high coherence pairs, `[low, high]`. Calculated as `[kMedian * 0.5, kMedian * 2.0]`, where `kMedian <= 0.5 && kMedian >= 0.12`.

### Decisions

- `selection`: The selected button value, `vc_l`, `sc_l`, `sc_r`, or `vc_r`.

### Timing

- `trialStart`: Timestamp of when the start of the trial is invoked.
- `trialEnd`: Timestamp of when the trial is ended and the next trial invoked.
- `trialDuration`: Total duration of the trial presentation.
- `decisionStart`: Timestamp of when the start of the dot direction decision is presented.
- `decisionEnd`: Timestamp of when the direction is selected and the selection is stored.
- `decisionDuration`: Total duration of the direction decision being presented and the participant making a selection.
- `motionDuration`: Total duration that the moving dot stimulus is presented for (default is `1500` milliseconds).

## Trial Structure

1. `initial`: Presenting a fixation cross for `1000` milliseconds.
2. `motion`: Presenting the dot motion for `trial.motionDuration` milliseconds (default `1500`).
3. `reference`: Presenting the dot direction decision, no time limit.
4. `decision`: Presenting a fixation cross for `250` milliseconds.
5. `confidence`: Presenting the confidence selection slider **if enabled on that trial**, no time limit.

## Notes

- Due to inaccuracies when using millisecond-precision timing alongside complex 2D rendering on the web, the result for `trialDuration` is typically between `65`-`75` milliseconds **greater** than the sum of other `-TotalTime` values and fixed durations.
